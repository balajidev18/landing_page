Common Questions

Everything you may want to ask

Is my gold safe with RAR Fincare?
Yes. Your jewellery is sealed in a tamper-proof packet in front of you, insured with {{INSURER_NAME}}, and stored in a secure vault. It is released back to you — with the seal intact — the moment you close the loan.
What documents do I need?
Only your Aadhaar card and PAN card. No income proof, no salary slips, no guarantors, and no CIBIL score check.
How is my jewellery valued?
On certified scales at the counter, in front of you, with an electronic purity check. You receive a printed valuation sheet showing the weight, purity, and eligible amount.
Are there prepayment or hidden charges?
No prepayment charges — close your loan any day. Interest is charged only for the days you actually use the loan. All applicable charges are shown in writing before you sign.
What happens if I can't repay on time?
We contact you well in advance and work out options — part payment, interest servicing, or renewal. Auction is always the last resort, done only after written notices as per RBI guidelines, and any surplus from auction is returned to you.
Can I release only part of my jewellery?
Yes. Pay down part of the loan and take back specific ornaments while the rest continue under pledge — ask at the branch for part-release.
